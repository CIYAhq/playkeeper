PaperMC logomark: terms of use

Source: https://assets.papermc.io/brand/papermc_logo.min.svg
Terms: https://docs.papermc.io/misc/assets (read 25 Sep 2026)

PaperMC publishes its logos for representing its software, including in
downloads, server selectors and similar places. Its terms ask that the logo
is not altered (colours, proportions or transparency), not combined with
other branding, not used to imply ownership or endorsement, and not sold as
part of other products without PaperMC's explicit permission.

Playkeeper shows the file unaltered, in its server type picker, to name
Paper. The logo remains PaperMC's and is not covered by Playkeeper's licence.
