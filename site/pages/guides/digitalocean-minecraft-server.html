{{template "h2n" (dict "ID" "ports" "N" 3 "Text" "Open the ports")}}

A Droplet has no Cloud Firewall until you add one, and a Cloud Firewall blocks everything you haven't allowed. Networking › Firewalls › Create Firewall: keep its SSH rule, add these as inbound rules for All IPv4 and All IPv6, then apply it to your Droplet.

{{template "h2n" (dict "ID" "copies" "N" 6 "Text" "Keep copies off the Droplet" "Toc" "Copies off the Droplet")}}

World › Backup rules › Copies somewhere else encrypts every backup on the Droplet, then sends it to S3-compatible storage or to another machine over SFTP. DigitalOcean's Spaces is S3-compatible, from $5 a month for 250 GiB: pick Other S3-compatible storage and enter its endpoint, like https://ams3.digitaloceanspaces.com, the bucket and an access key. A copy at another company, such as Backblaze B2 or Cloudflare R2 (both have presets), also survives a problem with your DigitalOcean account. Keep the recovery key somewhere other than the Droplet.
