{{template "h2n" (dict "ID" "what" "N" 1 "Text" "What a port forward does" "Toc" "What it does")}}

Friends outside your home reach your router's public address, not your PC. A port forward tells the router that connections to port 25565 go to one PC on your network. Without it the router has nowhere to send them, and friends get Connection refused or connection timed out.

{{template "h2n" (dict "ID" "ports" "N" 2 "Text" "The ports to forward" "Toc" "Which ports")}}

A new Minecraft 1.21.1 server's server.properties starts with these, and add-ons bring their own:

WhatPortForward it?
The server, server-port25565 TCPYes
Bedrock players, through Geyser19132 UDPOnly for Bedrock friends
Simple Voice Chat24454 UDPOnly if the server has it
Query, query.port25565 UDPNo: it's off unless enable-query is on
RCON, rcon.port25575 TCPNever: anyone with the password could run commands

A second server on the same PC needs a port of its own, like 25566, with its own forward.

Friends then join at your public address, followed by :25566 or whichever port when it isn't 25565.

{{template "h2n" (dict "ID" "cgnat" "N" 4 "Text" "When no forward can work" "Toc" "CGNAT")}}

Compare the internet address on your router's status page with the one curl -4 ifconfig.me prints. If they differ, something between your router and the internet shares one address among many homes, and connections from outside stop there:

  • A router address from 100.64.0.0 to 100.127.255.255 is carrier-grade NAT, the range set aside for it in RFC 6598. Ask your internet provider for a public IPv4 address, or host the server somewhere else.
  • An ISP box in front of your own router: forward the port on both, or set the ISP box to bridge mode.
{{template "h2n" (dict "ID" "home" "N" 5 "Text" "Joining from home, and keeping it safe" "Toc" "At home, and safety")}}
  • On your own network, join at the PC's local address. Some routers don't send a connection to their own public address back inside.
  • Forward only the game port, never RCON, and keep online-mode=true with an allowlist, so only friends you add can join.
  • Your public address can change. Many home connections get a new one now and then; check it again when friends can't join.
{{template "h2n" (dict "ID" "vps" "N" 6 "Text" "Or skip the router: a VPS" "Toc" "On a VPS")}}

A VPS has a public address and no router in front of it, so there's nothing to forward. Allow the port in the provider's firewall instead. Playkeeper's installer allows TCP 25565 in ufw or firewalld when one is on, each further server takes the next port from 25566, and friends join at a free name like survival.alex.playkeeper.me instead of an address that changes.
