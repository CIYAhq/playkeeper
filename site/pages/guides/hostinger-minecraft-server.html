{{template "h2n" (dict "ID" "copies" "N" 6 "Text" "Keep copies off the VPS" "Toc" "Copies off the VPS")}}

Hostinger's free weekly backups copy the whole VPS, and stay at Hostinger. For copies of each world that survive losing the VPS, turn on World › Backup rules › Copies somewhere else: every backup is encrypted on the VPS, then sent to S3-compatible storage or to another machine over SFTP. Hostinger doesn't sell S3-compatible storage, but Backblaze B2, Cloudflare R2 and Wasabi each have a preset. Keep the recovery key somewhere other than the VPS.
