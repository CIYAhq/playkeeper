{{template "h2n" (dict "ID" "copies" "N" 6 "Text" "Keep copies off the server" "Toc" "Copies off the server")}}

World › Backup rules › Copies somewhere else encrypts every backup on the server, then sends it to S3-compatible storage or to another machine over SFTP. Vultr's Object Storage is S3-compatible: pick Other S3-compatible storage and enter its endpoint, like https://ams1.vultrobjects.com, the bucket and an access key. A copy at another company, such as Backblaze B2 or Cloudflare R2 (both have presets), also survives a problem with your Vultr account. Keep the recovery key somewhere other than the server.
