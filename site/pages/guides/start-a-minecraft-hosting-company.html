{{template "h2n" (dict "ID" "how" "N" 1 "Text" "How it works" "Toc" "How it works")}}

Whop sells, and Playkeeper hosts. A buyer picks a plan on your store and pays on Whop's checkout. Playkeeper, on your VPS, makes their account inside the plan's memory and disk, and Whop's chat tells them it's ready. They sign in with their Whop account, create their server and play.

  • Whop does the store, checkout, renewals, receipts, taxes, refunds and payouts. Playkeeper never touches the money.
  • Playkeeper does the servers: each buyer's own memory, disk and share of the processor, in a locked-down container, with backups they can download.
  • When a plan ends, the buyer's servers stop. For 14 days they can still sign in and download their backups, and renewing picks up where they left off.

A Whop account, and a VPS big enough for the buyers you want. Your store starts with these plans, and you can change them:

Add up the plans you sell, and leave 2 GB of memory for the system, Docker and Playkeeper. A VPS with 16 GB of memory fits three Starter buyers, with 90 GB of disk for them. The sizing guide has the rest.

{{template "h2n" (dict "ID" "buyers" "N" 4 "Text" "What a buyer sees" "Toc" "What a buyer sees")}}
  • Right after paying, a message in Whop's chat says their server is ready: they sign in at your dashboard and create it, and it's up a few minutes later. If your VPS is full, the message says it's being set up, and another follows once there's room.
  • They sign in with Whop and look after their server themselves: plugins and mods, the console, players and backups, inside what their plan allows.
  • A second plan adds up: the same account gets the room of both.
  • Cancelling keeps the plan until the end of what they paid for, and Whop's chat reminds them before it ends.
{{template "h2n" (dict "ID" "costs" "N" 5 "Text" "What it costs, and who earns what" "Toc" "Costs")}}
  • The VPS is your main cost. What a Minecraft server costs compares providers.
  • Whop keeps a fee on each payment and pays you the rest: 2.7% + $0.30 for a domestic card, about 57¢ on a $10 plan, and 1.5% more for an international one.
  • Playkeeper is free and open source (AGPL-3.0). It earns 10% of Whop's profit on sales through stores made from its blueprint. That comes out of Whop's share, so it doesn't change your prices or payouts.

Checked on 29 Sep: the card fees in Whop's fees documentation, and the blueprint share in Whop's blueprint documentation.

{{template "h2n" (dict "ID" "name" "N" 6 "Text" "Your name, and Minecraft's rules" "Toc" "Your name")}}
  • Name the company your own way. Mojang's usage guidelines let a name say it's for Minecraft, as in "Kotoba Miners: A Minecraft server for Redstone builds", but not lead with Minecraft. The store comes without Minecraft's logo or art.
  • Write your own terms. The store has a template for them: fill in your company's name and how buyers reach you.
