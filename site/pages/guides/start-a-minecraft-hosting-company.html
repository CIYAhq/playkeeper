{{template "h2n" (dict "ID" "how" "N" 1 "Text" "How it works" "Toc" "How it works")}}

Whop sells, and Playkeeper Cloud hosts. A customer picks a plan on your store and pays on Whop's checkout. Playkeeper Cloud makes their account with the servers and memory the plan allows, and your Whop chat tells them it's ready. They sign in with their Whop account, create their server and play.

  • Whop does the store, checkout, renewals, receipts, taxes, refunds and payouts. Playkeeper never touches the money.
  • Playkeeper Cloud does the servers, on machines it runs: each customer's own memory, disk and share of the processor, in a locked-down container, with backups they can download.
  • You run the company: its name, its prices and its customers, who talk to you in your Whop chat. Playkeeper supports you.
  • When a plan ends, the customer's servers stop. For 14 days they can still sign in and download their backups, and renewing picks up where they left off.

Each plan's stock on Whop follows the room Playkeeper Cloud has for it, so Whop stops selling a plan while there's none.

{{template "h2n" (dict "ID" "buyers" "N" 4 "Text" "What a buyer sees" "Toc" "What a buyer sees")}}
  • Right after paying, a message in your Whop chat says their server is ready: they sign in from your store site and create it, and it's up a few minutes later. If Playkeeper Cloud needs a moment to make room, the message says it's being set up, and another follows once it's ready.
  • They sign in with Whop and look after their server themselves: plugins and mods, the console, players and backups, inside what their plan allows.
  • A second plan adds up: the same account gets the room of both.
  • Cancelling keeps the plan until the end of what they paid for, and your Whop chat reminds them before it ends.
{{template "h2n" (dict "ID" "costs" "N" 5 "Text" "What it costs, and who earns what" "Toc" "Costs")}}
  • Nothing up front: no VPS to rent, and nothing to pay before your first sale.
  • Playkeeper Cloud takes $8.50 a month for each 4 GB you sell, so $17 for Plus, as a revenue share of each payment, before Whop's fees. Whop shows it as a percentage of your price, such as 70.84% at $12 or 56.67% at $15, and Playkeeper Cloud sets it again when you change a price.
  • Whop keeps a fee on each payment and pays you the rest: 2.7% + $0.30 for a domestic card, and 1.5% more for an international one.
  • Keep the share as Playkeeper Cloud sets it. While it's gone or short, your store takes no new customers, and after three days Playkeeper Cloud stops hosting it.
  • Playkeeper also earns 10% of Whop's profit on sales through stores made from its blueprint. That comes out of Whop's share, so it doesn't change your prices or payouts.

Checked on 29 Sep: the card fees in Whop's fees documentation, and the blueprint share in Whop's blueprint documentation.

{{template "h2n" (dict "ID" "name" "N" 6 "Text" "Your name, and Minecraft's rules" "Toc" "Your name")}}
  • Name the company your own way. Mojang's usage guidelines let a name say it's for Minecraft, as in "Kotoba Miners: A Minecraft server for Redstone builds", but not lead with Minecraft. The store comes without Minecraft's logo or art.
  • Write your own terms. The store has a template for them: fill in your company's name and how buyers reach you.
