A game host costs more per GB, and you get more done for you: support to ask, a ready panel and protection against attacks. It's worth it for a public server with strangers joining, or when nobody in the group wants a VPS at all.

Realms is enough for a vanilla world with up to 10 friends at a time. For mods, plugins or more players, a VPS costs about the same, and every way to play with friends compares the rest.
