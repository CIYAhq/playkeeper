{{template "h2n" (dict "ID" "friends-list" "N" 2 "Text" "Add friends on Java: the friends list" "Toc" "Add friends on Java")}}

Since Minecraft 26.2, Java Edition has a friends list. It shows who's online and where they play.

{{template "steps" (dict "Items" (list (dict "Title" "Open the friends list" "Text" "Friends, on the title screen or the pause menu, or press O.") Each friend shows as Online, Online (LAN), Online (World), Online (Realm) or Online (Server). It can't take you into a friend's world yet: Mojang tested invites to singleplayer worlds in a 26.2 snapshot and took them out before release, and 26.3 doesn't have them either. To play together, pick one of the ways below.

Free and quick when everyone is in the same house. The world is open only while the host plays.

{{template "h2n" (dict "ID" "tunnel" "N" 4 "Text" "Friends somewhere else: a tunnel" "Toc" "A tunnel for friends elsewhere")}}

A tunnel gives the host's world an address friends can reach from home, with no router settings. It's free; the world runs on the host's PC and connection, and stops when they do.

{{template "h2n" (dict "ID" "realms" "N" 5 "Text" "Realms, run by Mojang" "Toc" "Realms")}}

Realms for Java costs $7.99 a month: you and 10 friends at once, always online, with backups. Friends play free.

{{template "steps" (dict "Items" (list (dict "Title" "Start a Realm" "Text" "Minecraft Realms, on the title screen. New subscribers get 30 days free.") (dict "Title" "Invite friends" "Text" "By name, or with an invite code, new in 26.3.") (dict "Title" "Friends join" "Text" "From their Realms list, or Join Realm with the code.") ))}}

Java Realms run no mods or plugins, only Java players can join, and it's 10 friends at a time.

{{template "h2n" (dict "ID" "free-host" "N" 6 "Text" "A free host")}}

Aternos and Minehut run a server for you, free. Aternos has a queue at busy times and stops the server when everyone leaves. Minehut's free plan has 1 GB of memory and 10 slots, and sleeps when empty. Fine for trying it out; outgrowing one is common, and moving an Aternos world takes minutes.

{{template "h2n" (dict "ID" "own-server" "N" 7 "Text" "Your own server, always on" "Toc" "Your own server")}}

Your own server keeps the world up when you log off, runs mods or plugins, and fits as many friends as it has memory for. On your PC it's free, but the PC stays on and you open port 25565 on your router. On a VPS it's always on, from {{usd $vps.USD}} a month: see what a Minecraft server costs.

The host and every friend need the same loader, Minecraft version and mods. The easy way is a modpack everyone installs in the same launcher.
