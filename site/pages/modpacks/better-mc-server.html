BMC5 adds dimensions, biomes and bosses, from The Aether and Oh The Biomes We've Gone to L_Ender's Cataclysm, the three biggest mods in it. Each new chunk costs more than in plain Minecraft, so:

  • Pre-generate the world before friends explore. In Playkeeper it's on the World tab.
  • Pick a VPS with fast cores: the sizing guide starts big modpacks at {{(sizingFor 4 "modpack").Cores}} cores.

On Playkeeper 0.4.3 or older, the first start stops. BMC5's own list of mods marks every mod as needed on servers, so two for players' games, BetterGrassify-1.6.1+neoforge.1.21.1.jar and distraction_free_recipes-neoforge-1.2.1-1.21.1.jar, go on and fail with NoClassDefFoundError: net/minecraft/client/ParticleStatus. Update Playkeeper, or delete both from mods on the Files tab and start it again.

{{template "h2n" (dict "ID" "manual" "N" 5 "Text" "By hand, from the pack" "Toc" "By hand")}}

18 of its mods come inside the pack rather than from Modrinth. The itzg Docker image, which Playkeeper runs servers in too, and mrpack-install both copy them. The Docker command leaves off the two mods that stop the server; with mrpack-install, delete them from mods before the first start.

{{template "modpack-docker" (dict "Pack" $m "Slug" "better-mc-neoforge-bmc5" "Exclude" "BetterGrassify,distraction_free_recipes")}}

Open TCP 25565 in your provider's firewall.
