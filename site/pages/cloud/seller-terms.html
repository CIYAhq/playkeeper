Playkeeper Cloud seller terms

For businesses that sell Minecraft server hosting on Whop and connect the Playkeeper Cloud app.

1. What these terms cover

Playkeeper Cloud ("Playkeeper", "we") runs Minecraft servers for businesses that sell hosting on Whop and connect our app ("sellers", "you"). These terms cover you as a seller. Your customers' use is covered by your own terms with them, and by Whop's.

2. What we do, and what you do

  • We run your customers' servers on our machines, keep their backups, and send them their messages, such as "your server is ready", in your Whop chat.
  • You sell the plans on your Whop store and set their prices. You're the merchant: payments, refunds, chargebacks and taxes on your sales are yours, under Whop's terms.
  • You answer your customers. Their questions go to you, in your Whop chat. We answer you (section 11).

3. Our fee

  • $8.50 a month for each 4 GB of server memory a plan allows, so $17 for 8 GB. It's on every payment, renewals included.
  • Whop pays it to us as a revenue share, taken from the full price before Whop's fees. Whop shows it as a percentage of your price. We change the percentage whenever your price changes, so it always comes to $8.50 per 4 GB.
  • Your price is at least $12 a month for each 4 GB. Plans renew monthly, in US dollars, with no free trial.
  • A customer's server starts only once their payment carried our share.
  • We give you [30 days'] notice before changing our fee. The new fee applies to payments after that.

4. Keeping our share in place

  • Open the store sets our share on each of your hosting products. Our app then keeps it matching your prices.
  • If our share is removed or lowered, your store stops selling at once, and your page says it needs a look. Customers who already have servers keep them.
  • If it stays that way for 3 days, or our app's access stays removed for 7 days, your store has left Playkeeper Cloud (section 7).

5. What your customers may run

  • Minecraft servers, within their plan's memory, disk and processor share.
  • Not: anything illegal where they, you or we are; attacks on others, such as floods of traffic, scanning or spam; cryptocurrency mining; malware; or sexual content involving minors.
  • You're answerable for how your customers use their servers. Put these rules in your own terms with them.

6. Suspending

  • We may suspend a customer, or your whole store, to stop abuse, to protect others or our machines, when the law requires it, or when our share isn't paid. Your page says so, and you can ask us why.
  • A suspended customer's servers stop, and they can't sign in.
  • A suspended store sells nothing, and its customers' servers stop until we lift the suspension.

7. Leaving

  • You can leave at any time, by removing our app or our share from your business on Whop.
  • When your store leaves, it stops selling, and each customer's plan ends: their servers stop and a final backup is taken; 14 days later, the servers are deleted; the final backup is kept 30 more days. We tell each customer in your Whop chat while we still can.
  • If you come back by opening our page in your Whop dashboard again, your store comes back closed until you open it.
  • We may stop offering Playkeeper Cloud to you with [30 days'] notice, or at once for serious abuse.

8. Your customers' data

  • We keep what running their servers needs: their Whop user and username, their plan, their servers and backups, and the messages we send them.
  • You see your customers' usernames, plans and status, and your earnings, on your page inside Whop.
  • No other seller sees your customers, and you don't see theirs.

9. What we don't promise

  • We work to keep servers running and backed up, but we don't promise they're never interrupted.
  • Our liability is limited to what your store paid us in the [3 months] before the claim.

10. Changes to these terms

We tell you on your page inside Whop [30 days] before new terms apply. Once they apply, selling through Playkeeper Cloud means you accept them.

11. Questions

  • Your customers: ask you, in your Whop chat.
  • You: ask us at [the seller support channel, to decide].
