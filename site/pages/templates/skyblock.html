{{template "h2n" (dict "ID" "needs" "N" 2 "Text" "Why it brings two more plugins" "Toc" "Why two more plugins")}}
  • VaultUnlocked: Iridium Skyblock won't load without Vault. Its Modrinth page doesn't say so, and our first check failed with "Unknown/missing dependency plugins: [Vault]". VaultUnlocked provides Vault.
  • WorldEdit: islands are .schem files, and its config pastes them with WorldEdit.
{{template "h2n" (dict "ID" "islands" "N" 3 "Text" "Islands, levels and upgrades" "Toc" "Islands and levels")}}

From the files Iridium Skyblock wrote on the test server:

  • Three island types, desert, jungle and mushroom, each with its own Nether and End island.
  • Islands sit 151 blocks apart in their own void world, called IridiumSkyblock, and PvP is off on them.
  • The Nether opens at island level 10 and the End at level 20. Islands level up as they earn experience.
