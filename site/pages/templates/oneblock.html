OneBlock doesn't make its islands on its own: the plugin's README has an operator create a world with its generator, then mark where the first block goes. In the Console, then in the game:

# In the Console: a world made with OneBlock's generator
mv create OneBlock normal -g Oneblock
# In the game, as an operator, standing in that world
/ob set 500

/ob set 500 puts the first block where you stand and spaces islands 500 blocks apart; without a number they're 100 apart.

{{template "h2n" (dict "ID" "play" "N" 3 "Text" "How players start and team up" "Toc" "How players start")}}
  • /ob takes a player to their own island, and a bar shows how far they are through the level.
  • /ob invite Alex asks a friend to share the island; they type /ob accept.
  • /ob listlvl lists the levels, from the first grass block to the End.
{{template "h2n" (dict "ID" "defaults" "N" 4 "Text" "Defaults to look at" "Toc" "Defaults")}}

From the config OneBlock wrote on the test server:

  • Protection is off, so players can walk onto each other's islands. /ob protection true keeps them home.
  • Nobody is sent to the OneBlock world on joining. /ob autojoin true starts players on their island as they arrive in it.
  • Islands are laid out in a circle, and the Nether is allowed. Both are in plugins/Oneblock/config.yml.
