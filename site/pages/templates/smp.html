{{template "h2n" (dict "ID" "pregen" "N" 3 "Text" "Pre-generate the world before friends explore" "Toc" "Pre-generate first")}}

New land is the heaviest work a server does. Generate it before the first evening together, so exploring doesn't lag for everyone. In the Console:

chunky radius 3000
chunky start

Or use Pre-generate on the World tab, which runs Chunky for you and pauses when the server runs low on memory.

{{template "h2n" (dict "ID" "griefing" "N" 4 "Text" "Undo griefing with CoreProtect" "Toc" "Undo griefing")}}
  • /co inspect, then click a block, shows who placed or broke it and when.
  • /co rollback u:Steve t:1h r:20 undoes what Steve changed in the last hour within 20 blocks.
  • /co restore with the same filters undoes a rollback.
{{template "h2n" (dict "ID" "voice" "N" 5 "Text" "Proximity voice chat" "Toc" "Voice chat")}}

Simple Voice Chat sends voice on its own port. Playkeeper opens UDP 24454 on the machine; open it in your provider's firewall too. Friends add the Simple Voice Chat mod to their game to talk, and can join without it to just play.
