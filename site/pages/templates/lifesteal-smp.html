From the settings LifeStealZ wrote when the test server started:

  • Everyone starts with 10 hearts, and can reach 20. A kill moves one heart from the victim to the killer.
  • A player on 0 hearts is eliminated and banned, until a friend revives them with a revive beacon or an operator types /revive.
  • /withdrawheart turns a heart into an item to trade or use later.
