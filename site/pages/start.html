What you need

A VPS, which costs money
Playkeeper is free, but you rent the server it runs on: from a few dollars a month for a few friends, more for big modpacks. Which size?
Ubuntu 24.04, for now
On an x86_64 machine with at least {{sizing.Min.Cores}} CPU cores, {{sizing.Min.MemoryGB}} GB of memory and {{sizing.Min.DiskGB}} GB of free disk. Other Linux systems and ARM machines aren't supported yet.
A computer for one step
You paste the command into the VPS from a terminal. After that, everything happens in the browser, on your phone too.
Minecraft: Java Edition
Paper, Purpur, Vanilla, Fabric, Quilt, NeoForge and Forge servers, as many as the VPS fits.
Nothing else to pay for
Free and open source (AGPL-3.0): every feature, with no ads, limits or trial.

About this page

This page, and no other on playkeeper.io, loads Whop's ad pixel, so our ads on Facebook and Instagram can tell who visits and who copies the install command. It sets a cookie, _wuid, on playkeeper.io for two years, and your browser then sends it with every request to playkeeper.io and the addresses under it: the rest of this site, and dashboards still on a free name from before free names moved to playkeeper.me. Dashboards under playkeeper.me never get it. It also keeps a fingerprint of your device in your browser, and sends Whop this visit with your browser's details, the ad's IDs from this page's address, how long you stay and whether you copy the command. Whop passes that on to Meta.

The pixel doesn't load if your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track. To remove what it stored, clear playkeeper.io's cookies and site data in your browser. The rest of playkeeper.io counts visits without cookies.
