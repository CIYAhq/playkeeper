About this page

This page, and no other on playkeeper.io, loads Whop's ad pixel, so our ads on Facebook and Instagram can tell who visits, who copies the install command or sends this page to their computer, and who opens the live demo. It sets two cookies: _wuid, a visitor ID kept on playkeeper.io for two years, and _wuid_link, which your browser then sends with every request to playkeeper.io and the addresses under it: the rest of this site, and dashboards still on a free name from before free names moved to playkeeper.me. Dashboards under playkeeper.me never get it. It also keeps a fingerprint of your device in your browser, and sends Whop this visit with your browser's details, the ad's IDs from this page's address, how long you stay, and whether you copy the command, send the page to your computer or open the live demo. Whop passes that on to Meta. The Send to my computer button shares this page's address as it is, ad IDs included, so Whop can tell a visit from your computer came from the same ad.

The pixel doesn't load if your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track. To remove what it stored, clear playkeeper.io's cookies and site data in your browser. The rest of playkeeper.io counts visits without cookies.
