What Pterodactyl and Pelican do better

Many games

Eggs cover hundreds of games. Playkeeper runs Minecraft: Java Edition only.

Selling servers

Users, limits and billing systems for hosting companies.

A big ecosystem

Years of eggs, themes and guides from the community.

Panel plugins

Pelican adds a plugin system to the panel itself.
